# Editorial standards

## Authorship and usefulness

- Chris owns the point, perspective and publication decision. Preserve audience, scope and emotional temperature. Do not infer his beliefs, preferences, credentials, relationships or product use from generic personas. Frustration must come from Chris and remain attached to the actual subject and incident he described. A reader problem does not authorize simulated author irritation.
- Establish what the reader should understand, decide or accomplish. The piece needs a contribution beyond restating current search results: explanation, synthesis, experience, working example, useful comparison or reasoned position.
- Keep complete useful explanations. Neither a length target nor a brevity preference justifies padding or cutting essential detail. Split a genuinely distinct reader task only with an intelligible continuation and owner alignment.
- No em dashes, emojis, canned hype, the phrase "no hype", performative authenticity, invented anecdotes or detector gaming. Concrete dislikes remain relevant; contraction percentages and sentence ratios are retired.
- Do not add a repeated conclusion or announce editorial intentions to the reader. Explain the subject directly. Match enthusiasm and criticism to the supplied position and evidence.

## Discovery without distortion

Inspect live reader questions, search results and existing site coverage before fixing an angle or recommending titles. A user-supplied topic stays the assignment unless the owner agrees to change it. Identify competing intent and whether an update is more useful than another URL; never duplicate an article merely by changing its slug.

Finalize the title, summary and takeaways after drafting. They must describe actual coverage and preserve important entities. Use a concise, descriptive title and a summary of at most 160 characters, including spaces, as Chris's house standard. The destination schema may impose additional limits. Google has no fixed meta-description character limit and may use other page text; a schema pass alone does not establish compliance with this house standard. Preserve original publication dates and do not fake freshness. Before proposing a new title for an established article, inspect available performance evidence.

Open with the concrete situation, question or purpose before any takeaway block. Use format-specific sections when they help. FAQs are optional and must add reader value, for example through useful answers or easier lookup, and stand alone without ambiguous pronouns. Merely repeating the body or chasing a search feature is not enough. Do not force a FAQ, key-term list, table or summary into every piece. Site-generated navigation should not be duplicated manually. Prefer descriptive, task-specific H2s using the words readers use for the subject. Preserve owner-selected headings that accurately describe their sections. Exact query matching and repeated keyword variants are unnecessary.

Use a table for a meaningful comparison or lookup across shared attributes, including a symptom-to-next-check map. Use numbered steps for sequence and paragraphs for reasoning or narrative. Remove a table that only boxes up prose or duplicates the sections below. Inspect actual mobile wrapping; do not shrink text to rescue it. Give each remaining table a private one-sentence purpose. [GOV.UK tables](https://design-system.service.gov.uk/components/table/), [W3C tables](https://www.w3.org/WAI/tutorials/tables/).

Original-source attribution, clear passages, meaningful links and useful visuals help readers assess and reuse the work. Do not promise SEO or AI citation gains from a template, special file, keyword quota or source count. Metrics have distinct meanings: impressions, citations, visits and meaningful reader actions are not interchangeable. Document period and coverage; inspect the article's publication/revision history and relevant outside events before explaining performance. Do not infer causation from a before/after chart.

Current baseline sources, checked October 4, 2026: [Google AI search guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/ai-optimization-guide), [helpful content](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/creating-helpful-content), [reviews](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/specialty/ecommerce/write-high-quality-reviews), [Bing AI reporting and clarity guidance](https://blogs.bing.com/webmaster/2026/2/Introducing-AI-Performance-in-Bing-Webmaster-Tools-Public-Preview/). Refresh volatile guidance when it determines a current recommendation; these links are not perpetual factual authority. Google stopped showing FAQ rich results on May 7, 2026 and removed their documentation on June 15. A FAQ remains optional and must provide reader value; remove redundant questions when their repetition adds no value. Do not add FAQ markup or FAQ sections for the retired feature. Bing's formatting suggestions do not make a FAQ mandatory. [Google updates](https://developers.google.com/search/updates), [Google headings and search vocabulary](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/seo-starter-guide), [Google snippets](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/snippet). Refresh these dated facts when needed.

## Promotion and gaps

Mention a product, internal article or commercial offering only where it adds specific reader value. Verify the actual offering and any claimed features, use honest contextual disclosure where needed, and preserve the topic. No promotion quotas, invented endorsements or thin purchasing advice.

Retain useful content gaps with a standalone proposed brief for owner review. Do not automatically queue or produce them. Distinguish research-completable ideas from ideas needing Chris's first-hand work. A future scheduler may consume confirmed briefs but must not upgrade ideas to approval.

## Quality and author control

Resolve known actionable defects before calling a candidate ready. Apply prose-review.md, including its automatic-F rules. Good source work, search packaging or a strong opening cannot offset scolding, useless contrast or invented author feeling elsewhere in the article. A reviewer must support a factual correction with evidence; subjective preferences do not override approved wording. A finite tool capability or missing owner input may leave a specific check pending. Report that boundary honestly rather than loop pointlessly or announce full completion.

Revisions to existing articles preserve original/current/proposed wording, URLs, assets and technical repairs. Protect supplied personal accounts. Missing independent corroboration is not permission to erase them or add public qualifiers. Confirmed hazardous instructions and credentials need the destination's applicable exception handling, never silent restoration.
