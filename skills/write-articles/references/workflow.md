# Workflow, review and delivery

## Private working record

Keep a confirmed brief and original request/corrections, research with the claim ledger, author-material references, outline when useful, complete draft, link decisions, visual plan/assets, reviews and approval state together. Use the project's private working directory. Helpers described in tooling.md supply the mechanical state/fingerprint interface. No new cloud store is required.

The brief records topic, angle, audience, destination, article type, reader outcome, original contribution, constraints and needed author input. It distinguishes an idea from a confirmed brief and may carry an external queue ID. Confirm once before production; resume confirmed work without repeating that gate. An evidence-driven change of premise or author position is a new decision, not a hidden brief edit.

## Sequential responsibilities

1. Research agent receives the full confirmed brief, original relevant corrections and applicable standards. It finds primary evidence and counterevidence, retains a claim ledger and useful sources, and reports actual access or author-input gaps.
2. Writing agent receives that complete context and research, reads the format and voice references, and produces a complete candidate, contextual links and visual plan. It cannot invent facts or personal experience to smooth gaps.
3. QC agent reads the actual candidate, independently extracts claims, independently searches/opens sources, checks procedure and reasoning, and returns concrete findings with evidence. Every factual assertion needs substantiated support; any omitted public citation needs the explicit rationale and underlying basis specified in research.md. Reject unsupported assertions and fabricated author experience before declaring the article ready for owner review. It does not receive instructions to endorse an intended verdict. Perform the full prose-review.md pass, not merely phrase matching. Quote each defective span, explain its reader cost and propose a scoped repair. Check headings, summaries, tables, prompts, captions and endings as well as the opening. Useless contrast and AI scolding automatically fail the candidate; invented author affect or experience also blocks delivery.
4. Coordinator adjudicates findings, makes scoped repairs and verifies affected dependencies. Fresh independent final reviewer then inspects the candidate for remaining unsupported, misleading, missing or weak material. Prior findings may be inspected but are not proof. Explicitly check that prose repairs persist throughout the article, remaining tables and FAQs earn their space, and summaries meet the house limit. Record legitimate technical distinctions, exact analytical quotations and necessary narrow warnings as contextual decisions, rather than banning every negative sentence.
5. Coordinator reads the complete final text, including metadata and captions, and inspects every image at reading size with surrounding text. Review desktop and mobile rendering when an integration candidate exists. Check code, tables, contrast/legibility, image labels and destination promises.

Do not combine these independent roles into one agent for convenience. Process multiple articles sequentially unless the owner requests a different arrangement. If a capability is unavailable, continue other useful work and retain the missing acceptance condition. Evidence-based disagreement needs adjudication; a reviewer preference cannot silently override author intent.

## Visual decisions

Use authentic screenshots for real interfaces and outcomes. Keep an image plan after the draft identifying each image's purpose, provenance and placement. Follow the destination's established hero style. No minimum image count, but assess the opportunity rather than skipping visual work automatically.

Prefer HTML-rendered diagrams for layouts where editable text and precise placement improve results; use the image-generation tool for generated raster illustration or image edits. Generated illustrations must be labelled accurately in private provenance and must not impersonate observed tests. Verify diagrams, labels and numbers against the article. Inspect assets and rendered output at actual reading size, including overlap, clipped labels, captions and alternative text. Missing owner-dependent visuals keep the relevant check pending. A file existence check is not image QA.

## Revision and finish

Repair an existing draft rather than regenerate it without reason. Record the substantive change and invalidate affected checks, including title/summary, claims, linked destinations and visuals. Preserve original/current/proposed text when changing existing public content. Keep author-approved text distinguishable from new proposals.

Do not stop at a known actionable defect because an iteration cap or aggregate grade passed. If progress depends on unavailable owner evidence or an external capability, report the precise unresolved item and continue independent work. Do not repeat unchanged audits in a loop.

Record recurrent mistakes and their actual cause in the private working record. Fix the artifact and propose a narrow reusable correction for owner approval; do not automatically add universal rules or rebuild the whole workflow after one failure.

Deliver the complete candidate first, then a compact checklist: brief/voice, every-claim review, internal destinations, visuals/rendering, metadata/site checks, specific unresolved inputs, evidence locations and actual per-phase and per-article usage if exposed. Do not invent metering. Reader-facing copy should not contain internal workflow labels.

Keep per-stage completion, pending inputs and the next executable step in the working record, alongside fingerprint checks. Use states honestly: working; awaiting specific input; ready for owner review; wording approved; integrated; published; cancelled. Resume the actual stage, preserve owner-controlled fields, and remove published work from active WIP after verified delivery. Publication requires its own authorization and live verification. Skill adoption also requires the owner's judgment of the private trials; passing helper checks does not establish good writing.
