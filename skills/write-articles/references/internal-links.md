# Internal links that earn their place

The goal is a useful next explanation or action at the point the reader needs it. Shared keywords and topical similarity alone are insufficient.

## Retrieve from actual content

Use the destination adapter to inventory actual article bodies, headings, metadata, valid anchors and source fingerprints. Consult published content, active drafts and queued briefs for overlap, but only eligible published destinations can become reader-facing links. Confirm live availability separately from local metadata.

Describe the passage's reader need before querying: prerequisite, focused explanation, diagnostic branch, worked example, evidence or useful next step. Use task/entity/version terms and natural alternative wording. Deterministic retrieval searches full content and returns evidence passages and source paths; it proposes candidates, not final links. Expand queries or inspect the wider inventory if results are weak. A title match cannot automatically outrank actual usefulness.

Read every proposed destination completely. Check its real audience, scope, versions, procedure and claims. If local text differs from the live destination, base the link promise on what readers can actually access. Record the snapshot/date used. Do not silently fix or rewrite the destination to make the link work.

## Decide and write

For each accepted link, retain a private decision record: source passage, reader need, destination URL/anchor, destination fingerprint, supporting destination passage, concrete added value and proposed introduction. Rejected candidates need only a concise reason when it explains a non-obvious choice.

Link when the destination directly supplies something useful the current passage intentionally does not repeat. Reject broad subject overlap, circular detours, duplicated explanations, wrong versions and empty commercial plugs. Zero links is a valid result after a substantive search. When none survive, expand beyond same-topic titles to prerequisites, worked examples and useful next steps, and record the actual inspected coverage and the gap privately. Do not mistake the first rejected shortlist for an exhaustive search. Do not add a link quota, automatic reciprocal links or forced cross-site promotion.

Use a descriptive phrase that belongs in the sentence. Explain the handoff only as much as necessary. For example, a deployment paragraph could say "If the service starts but drops its connection, use the reconnect diagnostics" only when the destination actually contains those diagnostics. Do not promise a complete setup guide when the page merely mentions installation.

## Verify after edits

Validate the actual URL and fragment, destination publication state and changed source fingerprints. Refresh changed destinations and reconsider their value. Inspect the rendered link placement and have the editorial reviewer judge its introduction in context. Mechanical validity cannot prove relevance or natural writing.

Live fetches are read-only. Treat redirects, login walls and blocked responses distinctly; a generic homepage redirect is not a valid article. If live access cannot be established, keep the selection pending or choose a verified useful alternative. Do not silently relabel a local draft as published.
